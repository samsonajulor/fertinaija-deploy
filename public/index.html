How it works

Guidance that stays with you.

FertiNaija is a separate support layer. We help you understand, prepare and check in. The facility remains responsible for every medical decision.

  1. 1
    Explore

    Choose what you want to understand and ask questions without uploading medical records.

  2. 2
    Check your location

    Confirm that you are currently in Nigeria. This does not prove identity or medical eligibility.

  3. 3
    Choose a destination

    Review factual public clinic information and decide where you want to make an independent enquiry.

  4. 4
    Check in when you arrive

    Message the FertiNaija support team on the platform. Your coordinator can help you prepare what to ask.

  5. 5
    Follow up

    Tell us how the visit went and get help understanding your non-medical next steps.

Straight answers

Before you continue.

Is FertiNaija a clinic or medical provider?+

No. FertiNaija is an independent information and coordination service. It does not diagnose, screen, recommend treatment or make medical decisions.

Will a clinic receive my information?+

No. FertiNaija does not send your personal information or conversation to a clinic. You decide whether to contact a facility and what to share.

Does completing the guide mean I qualify?+

No. The guide only helps you understand the general journey. A qualified facility determines medical and programme eligibility.

Can FertiNaija guarantee compensation?+

No. Compensation and reimbursement vary by programme and may not be available. Confirm current terms directly before making commitments or travelling.

Who can see my support conversation?+

The automated guide and authorised FertiNaija coordinators may access the conversation to provide support and maintain an audit trail. Do not send medical records, NIN or financial credentials.
