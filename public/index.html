01

Surrogacy

Private arrangements may include agreed support, expenses or compensation for the significant time and pregnancy commitment involved. Understand the medical and legal questions first.

02

Egg donation

Some private programmes reimburse expenses and compensate eligible donors for time, inconvenience, risk and commitment. Screening and medical consent still come first.

03

Sperm donation

Some active donor programmes compensate eligible participants. Availability, frequency and reward terms vary, so confirm the current programme before travelling.

Reward clarity

Donation does not necessarily mean unpaid.

Private programmes may reimburse expenses or compensate eligible participants for time, transport, meals, inconvenience, risk and commitment. Lilyfields Medical Center, for example, publicly describes reimbursements and time-and-commitment compensation for eligible egg donors.

Ask for the complete reward terms in writing. Confirm screening, risks and who covers medical costs. Never treat an enquiry as guaranteed acceptance or payment. Read the published programme example →

Straight answers

Before you continue.

Is FertiNaija a clinic or medical provider?+

No. FertiNaija is an independent information and coordination service. It does not diagnose, screen, recommend treatment or make medical decisions.

Will a clinic receive my information?+

No. FertiNaija does not send your personal information or conversation to a clinic. You decide whether to contact a facility and what to share.

Does completing the guide mean I qualify?+

No. The guide only helps you understand the general journey. A qualified facility determines medical and programme eligibility.

Is the opportunity expert a doctor?+

Not necessarily. “Opportunity expert” means an independent FertiNaija coordinator who can help you compare public information and prepare questions. It does not mean a doctor, lawyer or financial adviser.

Does donation mean I will not be rewarded?+

No. Some private programmes publish compensation or reimbursement for eligible participants. The amount, reason, timing and availability vary. FertiNaija helps you identify the questions to ask but cannot guarantee selection or payment.

Why does the directory show several clinics?+

FertiNaija does not automatically send you to one clinic. Choose your state to see public listings nearby, verify each programme directly and talk to an opportunity expert if you want help comparing the published information.

Who can see what I send on Telegram?+

The website guide stays in your browser and does not send your selections anywhere. If you continue to Telegram, the messages you send there may be seen by FertiNaija coordinators and are also subject to Telegram's privacy practices. Do not send medical records, NIN or financial credentials.
