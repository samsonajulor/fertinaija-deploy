Privacy notice

Your information stays under your control.

Effective 4 November 2026

Who this notice covers

FertiNaija is an independent fertility-opportunity information and coordination service hosted on QuikDB. It is not a clinic, medical provider or representative of any listed facility. The FertiNaija service operator is responsible for the information it deliberately receives through the website and its Telegram coordination channel.

Information processed on the website

  • Browser-local choices: the opportunity topic and state you choose in the guide stay in the current browser session unless you allow the optional location preference.
  • Consent and preference data: the site stores your privacy choice in one first-party cookie. If you allow preferences, it stores your selected directory state in this browser.
  • Technical data: hosting infrastructure may process IP address, browser and device information, request time and requested page to deliver and secure the website.
  • Telegram information: FertiNaija receives only the messages and account information you deliberately share after leaving the website for Telegram.

The website does not request precise geolocation, medical records, NIN, payment credentials, passwords or diagnostic information. Please do not send those items through the guide or Telegram.

Purposes and lawful use

Information is used to deliver and secure the website, remember choices you consent to, answer questions, coordinate non-medical support, address complaints and meet legal obligations. Optional preferences are based on consent and can be withdrawn at any time. Necessary technical processing supports delivery, security and legitimate service operations.

Cookies and browser storage

  • fertinaija_privacy_choice: necessary first-party cookie that records “essential” or “preferences” for up to 12 months.
  • fertinaija_directory_state: optional local-storage preference that remembers a selected directory location only after you choose “Allow preferences.” It remains until you clear browser data or switch to “Necessary only.”

FertiNaija does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies. Selecting “Necessary only” disables and clears the optional directory preference. The site also honours an enabled Global Privacy Control signal by using essential storage only.

Sharing, selling and external services

FertiNaija does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Listed clinics do not receive your profile, guide selections or Telegram messages from FertiNaija. QuikDB provides website hosting. Telegram, Google Maps and clinic websites process information under their own notices when you choose to open them. Review the Telegram Privacy Policy before sending information there.

Retention

The consent cookie lasts up to 12 months. Optional browser preferences remain until you clear them or withdraw consent. Hosting security logs are kept only for operational and security needs under QuikDB's applicable retention controls. Telegram messages follow the retention controls of Telegram and the FertiNaija coordination channel, subject to legal, safety and complaint-handling needs.

Your Nigerian data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or information about processing; object where applicable; and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

California privacy rights

California residents may request to know, correct or delete covered personal information, limit certain uses of sensitive information, opt out of sale or sharing, and exercise their rights without discrimination, subject to applicable exceptions. FertiNaija does not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. Global Privacy Control signals are treated as an essential-only preference. See the California Attorney General's CCPA information.

How to exercise a privacy choice or request

Use “Your privacy choices” in the website footer to change browser-storage consent. For an access, correction, deletion or other privacy request, contact the FertiNaija coordinator through t.me/fertinaija. FertiNaija may need enough information to verify that a request concerns you.

Children

The service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. FertiNaija does not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

Security and changes

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet service is completely secure. This notice may be updated when the service or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.
