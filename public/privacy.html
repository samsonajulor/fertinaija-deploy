Privacy notice

Your information stays under your control.

Effective 4 November 2026

About this service

FertiNaija is an independent information and coordination service hosted on QuikDB. It is not a clinic, medical provider or representative of any facility shown on the website.

What the website processes

The guided website experience processes your selected topic and state inside your current browser session. It does not upload that information unless you deliberately continue into the support-team chat and share it.

The support chat may collect information you submit, such as your name, contact details, general area of interest and visit status. Do not submit medical records, NIN, passwords, financial credentials or diagnostic information.

Why information is used

  • To respond to your questions.
  • To coordinate non-medical support before, during and after a visit.
  • To keep an authorised audit trail for safety, quality and complaint handling.
  • To understand and improve the website journey using aggregated analytics.

Clinic separation

FertiNaija does not send your profile, chat or personal information to a listed facility. If you contact a clinic, that interaction is directly between you and the clinic under its own privacy practices.

Human and automated access

An automated guide may respond first. Authorised human coordinators may review support conversations to help you and maintain the service record. The chat should not be described as end-to-end encrypted when authorised coordinators can access it.

Your choices

You can stop using the guide at any time, decline to share information and request access, correction or deletion of personal information held through the support channel. Use the support conversation to make a privacy request.

Important limitation

This notice covers FertiNaija only. External websites, map services and clinics have separate terms and privacy practices.
