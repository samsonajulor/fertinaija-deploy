Terms of use

Clear boundaries for independent support.

Effective 4 November 2026

Independent information service

FertiNaija provides general information, location references and non-medical coordination. It is not a healthcare provider, fertility clinic, employment service, agent or representative of any listed facility.

No medical or legal advice

Information from the website, automated guide or coordinator is not medical, legal or financial advice. A qualified facility and your independent advisers must explain the risks, procedures, eligibility requirements and legal consequences relevant to you.

No affiliation or appointment

A facility listing is based on public information. It does not mean the facility has joined, endorsed or authorised FertiNaija. The service does not book appointments or promise that a facility will see or accept you.

Possible rewards and reimbursements

Donation does not necessarily mean unpaid. Some private programmes publicly offer eligible participants reimbursement or compensation for expenses, time, inconvenience, risk or commitment. Each facility controls its own programme, eligibility and terms. Ask for current written details and independent advice before participating.

No guaranteed selection or compensation

Completing the guide, travelling to a facility or speaking with a coordinator does not guarantee eligibility, selection, treatment, reimbursement or compensation. Confirm current terms directly before making commitments or incurring travel costs.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information when you choose to share it.
  • Do not send another person's data without permission.
  • Do not submit medical records, government identity numbers, passwords or payment credentials through general chat.
  • Confirm a facility's current services and terms directly.
  • Seek independent professional advice before signing an agreement or undergoing a procedure.

Public information

Addresses, telephone numbers, service descriptions and reward terms may change. FertiNaija will correct a listing when reliable updated evidence is received, but cannot guarantee that every public detail remains current. A location-based result is a filter, not a medical recommendation or ranking.

Voluntary use

You may stop using the service at any time. If you believe there is an immediate health or safety emergency, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare provider rather than the website assistant.
